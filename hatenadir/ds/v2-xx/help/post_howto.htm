Uploading a Flipnote to Jeraldmemo!

1. You press the "Upload" button below and Selecting a Flipnote from your Flipnote Studio storage area (System Memory, SD Card)

2. Then you read the Terms of Service and agree to the posting of your Flipnote!

3. Then you select to Unlock/Lock your Flipnote to who can edit it and press "Post" and your Flipnote will be uploaded!
